Where to start

  1. Open the Construction & Fitting Guide: it shows you how to print the patterns and how to take measurements.
  2. Choose a pattern from the collection and download it as A4 tiled sheets (to print at home) or as a Plotter file (one full-size sheet).
  3. Print at 100 % (“Actual size”) and measure the test square: it must be 10 × 10 cm (3.94 × 3.94 in).
  4. Cut your first test toile before using your final fabric.
